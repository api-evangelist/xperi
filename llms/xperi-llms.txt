# Xperi

> Xperi Inc. (NYSE: XPER) is an entertainment technology company whose licensed technologies power audio, video, and media discovery experiences across connected cars, consumer electronics, pay-TV, and streaming media platforms. Its brands include DTS immersive audio, HD Radio digital broadcast radio, IMAX Enhanced certification, and the TiVo video platform.

Xperi is a technology licensing company: it ships its DTS, HD Radio, IMAX Enhanced, and TiVo technologies to automakers, device makers, and video service providers through commercial licensing and integration programs rather than a public developer API. As of 2026-07-21 no public developer portal, API documentation, OpenAPI specification, or /llms.txt was found on xperi.com or its brand properties (developer.tivo.com, developer.dts.com, and developer.xperi.com do not resolve). Its TiVo engineering organization maintains an open-source presence on GitHub.
